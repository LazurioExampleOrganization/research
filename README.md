# Lazurio Example Organization research

Module for mechanism scans, sources, market maps and evidence before deciding
what to test.

Research looks for projects that actually make money, breaks down their
monetization mechanism and proposes a legal adaptation for Lazurio Example
Organization. The goal is not to copy a company, but to understand its
economic engine.

## Minimum mechanism record

- project name and URL
- sources and verification date
- monetization signal and confidence
- customer, offer, channel, pricing and delivery
- what can be legally adapted
- what must not be copied
- legal, ToS and reputational risks
- proposal for the smallest test
